Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how "What If Physics Uploader" (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @whatif.physics in Germany, handles data. The App is used only by its operator for the TikTok account @whatif.physics.

1. Data the App accesses

When the operator connects his TikTok account through TikTok's official login, the App receives an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name, profile picture). With the operator's permission it uploads videos as drafts and reads basic statistics of the operator's own videos (for example view counts).

2. Purpose

The data is used only to upload the operator's own videos as drafts and to show the operator how his videos perform. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and service providers

Access tokens are stored by the integration service Composio, which handles the connection to TikTok on the operator's behalf. Video statistics are stored on the operator's own computer. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

4. Other TikTok users

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users.

5. Deletion

The operator can disconnect the App at any time in the TikTok settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data and to complain to a data protection authority.

7. Contact

Please contact us through the TikTok account @whatif.physics.
